FAQ

I hear no sound.Check the silent switch, the volume, and your headphones. If Hold or Loop is running, stop it and touch again.
How do I restore my purchase?Open Menu, go to the Unlock tab and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple account you bought with.
My saved sounds are gone.Saved data lives only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
Which devices are supported?iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The app is available in Japanese and English.
