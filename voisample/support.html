Voisample Support

FAQ

I hear no soundCheck the silent switch, the volume, and your headphones.
Also check that Mute is not on in the Pads screen. If a loop is running, press "Stop all sounds" and try again.
My recording does not turn into textCheck that the recording language (Japanese, English or Auto) matches your voice. Recording in a quiet place, close to your mouth, helps. Singing and loud background music may not turn into text. The first time you use a language, an internet connection may be needed to download the language data.
How do I restore my purchase?Open Info, tap "See details" on the Voisample Pro card, then tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple account you bought with.
How do I export my performance?Pro is required. Open the performance under Performances in the Library, tap the WAV save button, then choose "As recorded" or "Volume evened out".
Are my recordings sent to anyone?No. Your recordings and text never leave your iPhone. MarbLabo (the developer) has no server, so we cannot receive or see them.
My saved data is goneRecordings and pads live only on your device. Deleting the app removes them. Export what matters to you as WAV and keep a backup.
Which devices are supported?iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The app is available in Japanese and English.
